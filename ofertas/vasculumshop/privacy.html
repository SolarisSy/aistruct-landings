Privacy Policy

Last updated July 1, 2026.

Vasculum ("we," "us") publishes an editorial journal at vasculum.shop. This page explains what information we collect from visitors, why, and what choices you have. We keep this simple on purpose because we collect very little.

What we collect

If you subscribe to our newsletter through the form on this site, we store the email address you provide so we can send you journal updates. We do not require an account, a name, or any other personal detail to subscribe.

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical data for every visit — IP address, browser type, and pages requested — used only for security and to understand which entries are being read. We do not use this log data to build individual visitor profiles.

What we don't collect

  • We do not require registration to read the journal.
  • We do not sell or rent any information to third parties.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children.
  • We do not collect payment information anywhere on this site.

Cookies and local storage

This site may use your browser's local storage to remember that you've already subscribed to the newsletter, so the confirmation message doesn't reappear unnecessarily. This value stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

How your email is used

Newsletter addresses are used solely to send journal updates. We do not share subscriber lists with any outside party. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and doing so removes your address from future mailings.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send, or by writing to us directly. You can also ask us to confirm what information, if any, we hold about your email address, and ask us to delete it.

Data retention

We keep subscriber email addresses only as long as you remain subscribed, and technical server logs for a limited period before they are rotated out in the ordinary course of hosting maintenance.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove your information, can be sent to hello@vasculum.shop.
