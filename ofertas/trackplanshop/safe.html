About this journal

Trackplan started as a shared notebook between a handful of hobbyists comparing sketches of spare-room layouts. It's still that, just typed up. We write about the decisions that matter at small scale — radius, sidings, benchwork, and the timetable that makes a layout worth operating — and we try to say when we're not sure.
