About Ledgerline

A small, independent journal about keeping a household ledger by hand, written by people who actually keep one.

What this journal is

Ledgerline started as a shared notebook between a few households comparing how they kept track of money on paper — what columns they used, how they closed the month, what they wrote in the margins. It became a small public journal because the comparing turned out to be useful on its own. It is not a financial advice service, and it does not evaluate anyone's personal finances. It is a record of method: how a paper ledger is kept, and what tends to make one easy to trust a year later.

Who writes it

Ledgerline is edited by a small, informal group rather than a single author. Entries are written and checked by whoever on the team last closed the relevant month in their own ledger, which is part of why the voice can shift slightly from post to post.

Content policy

  • Every post is written for this journal and checked against the ledger it describes; none are syndicated or lightly reworded from elsewhere.
  • Nothing on this site is sponsored, and no entry recommends a specific product or service.
  • Column Book, our companion app, is mentioned openly where relevant and is not sold or advertised as a purchase inside any article.
  • When a figure in a piece is illustrative rather than an exact record, we say so in the text rather than presenting it as a verified fact.
  • Corrections are noted at the bottom of a piece rather than edited in silently.

Get in touch

Questions, corrections, or a method you think we should cover: write to hello@ledgerline.shop. We read everything, though replies can take a couple of weeks around a monthly close.
