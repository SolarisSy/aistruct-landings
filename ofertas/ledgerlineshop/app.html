Column Book

The same two-column habit this journal writes about, kept on a phone between paper sessions. Column Book is not sold here and it does not replace the ledger — it is the pocket version of it.

What It Does

7 features
No. 01

Two Columns, Not One

An entry column sits beside a running balance column, the way the paper version does, so a skipped line or a doubled entry tends to show up as a broken balance instead of hiding in a long list.

No. 02

A Guided Monthly Close

A short screen walks through the same steps as a paper close — total in, total out, carry the balance forward — instead of leaving the month open indefinitely.

No. 03

Categories You Set Once

A short, fixed list of headings you choose yourself. Nothing is auto-categorized, and nothing is renamed behind your back between months.

No. 04

A Margin for Notes

A short note field sits beside the entry for context — who it was for, why it was split — without folding that context into the number itself.

No. 05

A Page You Can Print

Export a plain page formatted like a ledger sheet, with a blank margin included, for readers who still copy entries into a physical book at the end of the week.

No. 06

Works Without a Connection

An entry saves on the device first. Recording something at the register does not depend on a live connection.

No. 07

One Shared Ledger

Invite one other person to the same ledger with a short activity note on each entry, rather than reconciling two separate lists later.

End of feature list

How It Works

5 steps
Step 1

Start a ledger

Open a new ledger or continue one you already have, and pick the categories you plan to use.

Step 2

Add entries as they happen

Date, description, amount. The running balance updates itself, the same as a second column would on paper.

Step 3

Add a margin note when it helps

A short note beside the entry, kept separate from the amount itself.

Step 4

Close the month

Work through the guided close, check the totals line, and carry the balance forward.

Step 5

Export or carry forward

Print a ledger-formatted page if you keep a paper copy, or simply carry on to the next month.

A Few Questions

mini-FAQ
Q1

Does Column Book replace a paper ledger?

Not by design. It is meant to sit alongside one, for entries that happen away from the notebook. See the full FAQ for how readers here tend to combine the two.

Q2

Is anything sold inside the app?

No. There is no store, no upsell screen, and nothing to purchase from within Column Book.

Q3

Can two people use one ledger?

Yes, with an activity note on each entry so it stays clear who logged what.

Q4

What happens if I skip a few days?

Nothing is lost. You can add entries out of order and the running balance recalculates from the dates you enter.

Column Book is in a small private trial — the Journal remains the main record

About availability

Column Book is a small, ongoing project of this journal, currently in a limited private trial rather than a public release. The pages here describe how it is meant to work; the writing on the Journal does not depend on it and stands on its own.
