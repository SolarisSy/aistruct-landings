About this journal

Ledgerline is written by a small group of people who keep their household accounts by hand and compare notes on method. Nothing here is sold, and none of it is financial advice for your particular situation — it is a record of what has worked, and sometimes not worked, at one kitchen table. Read more on the About page, or start with the Glossary for the terms used throughout the journal.
