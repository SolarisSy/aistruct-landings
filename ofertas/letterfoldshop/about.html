About Letterfold

Letterfold is an independent journal about the physical letter — the fold, the weight of the stock, the seal, and the etiquette of writing someone back. We don't sell paper or stationery. We write about the habit of using them.

How this started

Letterfold grew out of a shared notebook three friends kept while mailing each other during a long stretch of living in different cities. Somewhere around the fortieth letter, the notes about *how* we were writing — the fold, the paper, the timing of a reply — became more interesting than the letters themselves. The journal is what's left of that notebook, tidied up and made public.

"We weren't trying to start a publication. We were trying to figure out why some replies felt easy to write and others sat on the desk for three weeks." — from the original shared notebook, 2025

What we cover, and what we don't

Every entry on Letterfold is about the physical mechanics or etiquette of written correspondence: paper, folding, sealing, ink, envelopes, and the timing and manner of replying. We do not review or sell specific stationery brands, and we do not publish sponsored posts. If a piece mentions a material or technique, it's because it's relevant to the fold or the seal being discussed — not an endorsement.

Content policy

  • Every article is written for this site — nothing is copied or lightly rewritten from elsewhere.
  • We correct factual errors openly; a note is added to the bottom of the entry when something is fixed.
  • We don't accept payment in exchange for coverage of a product, brand, or technique.
  • Reader mail and corrections are welcome and read by an actual editor, not a queue.
  • Reply Tray is covered on the same editorial terms as everything else on the site.
