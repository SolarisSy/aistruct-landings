About Railstile

We write about the drawing, not the finish.

Railstile started as shared notes between a few people who kept seeing the same mistake in different shops: a project that went wrong not at the bench, but on paper, hours or days before the first cut. This journal is where those notes turned into something worth reading regularly.

Founded 2026 · Editorial Desk

Our Mission

We publish specific, measured writing about furniture architecture and woodworking design: how a dimension is decided, how a face is chosen as a reference, how a carcase is checked for square before glue-up. We are not a tool review site or a project-plan store. We're closer to a set of shop notes that happen to be public.

Every article starts from a real decision point — a place where two reasonable choices produce different results down the line — and tries to explain why one holds up better than the other.

The Desk

A small editorial team

Railstile is edited by a small group who split time between shop work and writing. Bylines are shared under the desk rather than attributed individually, since most pieces get read and revised by more than one person before they run.

Content Policy

  • Articles describe general shop method and design reasoning. They are not project plans, purchase advice, or instructions tied to a specific commercial product.
  • We do not accept paid placement inside articles. Any relationship to the Cutlist app is disclosed plainly, as it is on this page and on the app's own page.
  • Claims about measurement or material behavior are written as tendencies observed in shop practice, not as universal guarantees — wood and joinery outcomes vary by material, tool, and hand.
  • We correct published pieces when a reader points out an error, and we note the correction rather than silently editing it away.
  • We do not publish personal data about readers who write in; questions are answered privately unless permission is given to share them.
