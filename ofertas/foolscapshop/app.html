What Boxlist keeps track of

6 core tools

Box Ledger

Log every box with a call number, a physical location, and a one-line note on what's inside — searchable later without opening the lid.

Series Builder

Group folders and boxes into a series by function or by when they arrived, instead of forcing everything under a subject heading.

Retention Reminders

Set a retention window when a box is logged, and Boxlist flags the series once it's time to review — not before, not silently forever.

Disposal Log

Record what left the archive, when, and why — a short line is enough, but it turns "I think we shredded that" into an actual answer.

Shelf Map

Note which shelf, closet, or storage unit a box lives on, so "where did we put it" stops being the first question during tax season.

Offline-First

The ledger lives on your own device by default. An account is only needed if you want the same list on a second device.

How it works

Four steps

  1. Pick a starting number

    Most households start at 001 and let the number climb with each new series — no need to plan the whole scheme in advance.

  2. Log the first box

    Give it a call number, a shelf, and a short description. Boxlist timestamps the entry automatically.

  3. Set a retention window, if it needs one

    Tax records, warranties, and school papers each tend to have their own household rule. Attach it once and forget it.

  4. Review when flagged

    When a series comes up for review, decide to keep, renumber, or log its disposal — the ledger keeps the history either way.

A few questions

Mini FAQ

Is Boxlist required to follow along with Foolscap?

No. Every method described on Foolscap works with a notebook and a pencil. Boxlist just keeps the same ledger tidier and searchable.

Does it scan or store the documents themselves?

No. Boxlist tracks the ledger entry — call number, location, dates — not the contents of the page. It's an index, not a scanner.

Who is Boxlist meant for?

Households with a few boxes to a full closet of paper who want to know what's where without rebuilding the system from scratch every year.

Read the method first

Boxlist follows the same order-of-arrival approach we write about on the journal. Start with the entries, then decide if a ledger app is worth adding.
